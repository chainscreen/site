Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-12

This policy covers the ChainScreen website at chainscreen.io. ChainScreen now publishes compliance research and editorial material only. The former API, hosted checker, account system, lead forms, Terms of Service, and Data Processing Agreement were retired on 2026-05-12.

1. Who we are

DailyMind LTD (HE 439959), registered in Limassol, Cyprus, operates chainscreen.io. For privacy inquiries: info@chainscreen.io.

2. Data we collect

Website analytics -- We use privacy-focused, cookieless analytics to understand aggregate page views, referrers, browser type, country, and basic page performance. These analytics are used to improve editorial coverage and site performance.

Analytics providers -- The site may load self-hosted Umami analytics, self-hosted GoatCounter analytics, and Cloudflare Web Analytics. These tools are configured for aggregate measurement rather than user-level profiling. With your consent, we also load Microsoft Clarity for heatmaps and session recordings; it is not loaded unless you select “Accept Clarity”.

Cloudflare -- Cloudflare provides CDN, DNS, and security services. Cloudflare may process server logs such as IP address, user agent, timestamps, and request metadata for security and performance. Privacy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Email -- If you email us directly, we store your email address and message so we can respond. We do not add you to a mailing list without separate consent.

3. Cookies and local storage

The current ChainScreen website does not set analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics localStorage. The retired API, hosted checker, account system, and lead forms have been removed.

4. Legal basis for processing

  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) -- site security, CDN operation, aggregate cookieless analytics, and responding to direct inquiries.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) -- retaining records where required by applicable law.

7. Your rights under GDPR

If you are in the EU/EEA, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection to legitimate-interest processing, or withdrawal of consent. Email info@chainscreen.io. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children

The website is not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Supervisory authority

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection: www.dataprotection.gov.cy.

10. Data Protection Officer

We have not appointed a DPO because the website does not engage in large-scale systematic monitoring or processing of special categories of data. For privacy inquiries, contact info@chainscreen.io.

11. Automated decision-making

The website does not perform automated decision-making or profiling about visitors.
